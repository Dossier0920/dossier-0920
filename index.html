OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVE

Several confirmed attendees maintain known or suspected connections to foreign intelligence, diplomatic channels, private collections, and international transport networks.

At this time:

  • the nature of the item to be transferred is unknown;
  • the intended recipient has not been identified;
  • the method of transfer remains unclear;
  • no criminal act has yet been confirmed.

Because the event is masked, positive visual identification may be unreliable. All available surveillance imagery should be compared against the secure digital guest registry, sponsor credentials, and admission records.

Agents are instructed to observe, document, and identify all unusual contacts or handoffs.

Do not assume that legitimate admission indicates legitimate purpose.
